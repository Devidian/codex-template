---
name: feature-acceptance
description: Use when a completed feature is being prepared for human acceptance and merge.
---

Read `docs/development/testing.md` and the active phase. Run full required validation once application tooling exists. Check changed-line coverage is at least 90%, report overall and branch coverage separately, and identify critical uncovered paths. Summarize delivered scope, architecture, tests, limitations, and risks in the PR. Wait for explicit human acceptance before squash merge.
